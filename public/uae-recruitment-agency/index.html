FAQs

Questions we get asked a lot

How fast will I see CVs?

For most roles, you get a shortlist of 3 to 5 pre-screened CVs within 48 hours of your brief. Senior and niche roles may take longer; your consultant will give you a timeline on the first call.

Can the staff work on TASC's visa?

Yes. With contract staffing, TASC employs the staff, sponsors their visas and runs payroll, while they work for your team day to day. You don't need spare visa quota.

Can you handle volume hiring?

Yes. We hire from 1 to 500+ people, including fast ramp-ups such as 50 call-centre agents in 1.5 weeks for an e-commerce client.

Do you help with Emiratisation targets?

Yes. We source, screen and place UAE nationals in real roles, so your hires count towards your targets and stay.

Is TASC licensed and certified?

Yes. TASC operates from offices in Dubai and Abu Dhabi and manages all hiring in line with MOHRE requirements. Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015.

What it is

Bothwise is TASC's AI company.

A separate entity that acts on its own, endorsed as A TASC Company. It pairs human judgment with machine capability: people decide where AI should work, the system does the work at scale, and people prove the result.

The promise

Proof over promises.

We pick the number that matters before we build, deploy against it, and show the delta between the old process and the new. The metric changes with every client. The discipline of proof does not.

The thinking

Every AI brand you will meet this year sells the machine. We built the opposite.

Contrarian by design, human by conviction. The name comes from two halves that only work together.

The human half

  • First principles, not automation. Automating an existing workflow can raise costs without moving a meaningful needle. We rethink the workflow instead.
  • The number that matters. A project can look successful without changing a metric that counts. We pick the metric first and show a clear delta.
  • The doorman fallacy. Saving hours is worthless if the freed time sits idle. The goal is better business outcomes, never removing people.
  • Bottlenecks, not busywork. Optimising random steps strains an already clogged system. Judgment means knowing where to intervene.

The machine half

  • Trained, not templated. Off-the-shelf agents are not where the value is. Almost all our effort goes into training: institutional knowledge, skills, fine-tuned behaviour.
  • Always learning. A system that stops learning at launch falls behind. Ours keeps changing with the business, and users alter the agent on the fly.
  • The last mile. Forward-deployed engineers work inside the client's daily operations. Delivery is not a handoff.
  • Measured after go-live. Performance is optimised with quantitative methods. The output is priority actions, never another dashboard.

Why the name

  • Both. Human expertise and machine capability, working together. The advantage is the pairing, and the name says so.
  • Wise. Judgment: knowing where AI creates real value, and proving the result.
  • A word English forgot to have. Likewise, otherwise, clockwise. Bothwise reads as if it has always existed, which makes it easy to say, spell and remember.
  • Room to grow. One brand that spans consulting, AI services, data services and products such as Hyrra.

What happens next

  1. Trademark opinion. Screening in the UAE, US, UK and EU before any external use.
  2. Design exploration. The name is final. The shapes in the animation explain where it comes from; the logo itself is still being designed.
  3. Domain and channels. bothwise.ai is secured. bothwise.com and the social handles follow.
  4. Launch. Timing and rollout will be shared once the identity work is complete. Until then, the name stays internal.
